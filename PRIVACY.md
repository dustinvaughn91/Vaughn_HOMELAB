# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

## Overview

K.E.R.N.E.L. (Kernel), including Kernel Calendar, is a privately operated personal and family homelab application. It is not offered as a public or commercial service. Access is limited to authorized users.

## Google User Data

Kernel may request access to a Google account only to provide functionality authorized by the account holder, such as Gmail and Google Calendar integration. The application uses that access to perform requested operations, including reading authorized information and creating or deleting calendar events when an authorized user asks it to do so.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated commercial purposes. Access is limited to the application, its operator, and authorized users as needed to provide the requested personal or family functionality.

## Credentials and Security

OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and other secrets are stored outside the public source repository and are not intentionally published or committed to Git. Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to limit access, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

## Revoking Access

The account holder can revoke Kernel's Google account access at any time through the security or third-party access settings of the applicable Google Account. Revoking access may disable Gmail, Calendar, or other Google-connected features.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the private project evolves. Material changes will be published in this repository.
